## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished isolated 3D hero illustration
for a Ukrainian music party game website. A single sculptural vinyl record
tilted in three quarter perspective, wrapped by one swooping liquid chrome
ribbon that ends in a dynamic upward arrow, expressing music and two competing
teams. Record is glossy near-black with finely etched grooves, bright magenta
central label without any lettering. Ribbon is mirror chrome with iridescent
orchid pink highlights on the left and electric periwinkle blue highlights on
the right. A few tiny glossy orbital spheres, carefully restrained. Premium
high end music festival art direction, physically convincing polished material,
strong clean silhouette, crisp studio rim light, substantial dimensional depth.
Centered composition with entire sculpture fully in frame and generous margin.
No lettering, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no rectangular backdrop.
Truly transparent background, suitable for placing on a deep ink violet website.
One cohesive object, not a scene or UI.
