## Остаточний промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready transparent 3D illustration for the final screen of a Ukrainian home party game, next to very large victory typography. Create ONE beautifully designed sculptural trophy: a chrome silver cup with thick rounded pink-magenta handles and a luminous lavender interior, on a small glossy deep-purple circular pedestal. A sculptural four-point star is integrated into the cup front. Subtle small floating lavender and pink polished spheres and 2 angular chrome starbursts frame it closely. Premium playful music-show art direction, tactile smooth metal, clean specular highlights, believable studio reflections, a little pink rim light on the left and periwinkle rim light on the right. Three-quarter camera, slightly tilted trophy as a single coherent arrangement, centered and filling 85% of a square composition; all objects fully within frame. This should harmonize with a dark near-black plum UI, pink #ed8dc4, lavender #c3a5ee, periwinkle #a9b8ff and occasional pale lime accent. Restrained editorial 3D product render, high visual clarity when displayed at 180-400 pixels wide, no clutter. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no background rectangle, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. No gold, no sports logos, no cartoon faces, no scene, no ground plane.
